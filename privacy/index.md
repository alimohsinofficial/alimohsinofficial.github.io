# Privacy and site data

Canonical page: https://alimohsinofficial.github.io/privacy/

Author: Mohsin Ali. Content snapshot: 2026-10-14.

This is a static portfolio. It has no account system, analytics script, advertising tracker, or portfolio-operated backend.

## Preferences and browser details

Light/dark preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage. Studio and Tactical keep separate preferences. Tactical displays browser, viewport, language, time-zone, and device information locally. The site does not send those readouts to a portfolio server.

## Optional location lookup

Public-IP lookup runs only when you choose its button. It contacts [GeoJS](https://www.geojs.io/privacy/), which receives your IP address and returns an approximate location. Device location runs only after a separate action and your browser’s permission. Coordinates are used for the current globe display and are not saved by the portfolio.

## Contact form

The contact form sends your name, email address, subject, and message directly to Google Forms. The form owner can access submitted responses through Google Forms. Google handles processing, verification, and confirmation under [its privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Do not include passwords, payment information, or other secrets in a message.

## Hosting and links

GitHub Pages serves the public files and may process requests, including IP addresses, under [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). External sites have their own policies. The visualization libraries are hosted with this site.
