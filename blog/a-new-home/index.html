A new home for the work, and the thinking behind it

A portfolio rebuild that makes space for writing, while keeping the engineering work in view.

Mohsin Ali14 Oct 20262 min readAI-assisted

A portfolio that leaves room for thinking

This site brings together Mohsin Ali's engineering work, research, and professional background. The rebuild also gives writing a permanent home: a field notebook for AI, software systems, deep-tech research, and the process of building things.

The notebook is intended to grow alongside the work. Project pages provide the practical context; the resume brings the career history together; research pages connect to the original publications.

Two ways to explore

Studio keeps a fixed profile area on the left and scrolling content on the right, with flat sections and GSAP motion. Tactical is a separate full-screen military HUD: content changes in place, details open in dialogs, and the spherical globe is a small optional widget alongside the clock and visitor readouts. Mobile navigation sits at the bottom. Switching experiences retains the selected section or detail. Both have light and dark appearances. Public IP and approximate location lookup are visitor-initiated.

Built to stay simple

The site is static. Pages use folder-based URLs, and the interface uses the local Blueprint UI web component library. Articles are directly editable HTML pages. Writing can be prepared with AI, reviewed, and added without a compiler, package manager, server, or database.

What comes next

Planned topics include interpretable industrial AI, agent-assisted software development, research moving into production, and lessons from game and web development. Future articles will distinguish measured results from hypotheses and include useful source links where available.

This opening note was prepared with AI assistance from the portfolio brief. Project figures and career details elsewhere on the site are based on the supplied resume and are pending the owner's next update.
