## Context

An AI compressed-air monitoring platform for UK SME manufacturers, developed within Quantonova's R&D portfolio.

## The work

- Detect compressed-air leaks, equipment degradation, and scheduling waste using a Random Forest classifier.
- Engineer 15 temporal features, with SHAP explainability and forecasting.
- Deliver a SCADA-style dashboard on Google Cloud Run in europe-west2.

## Reported outcomes

**0.99** Classifier F1

**15** Temporal features

**~£1,900** Estimated annual recoverable savings / site

Figures are reported in the supplied resume and describe project validation, rather than guarantees for a new installation. The resume also reports 1% false positives, zero false negatives, and approximately 2 tonnes of annual CO₂ reduction per site.
